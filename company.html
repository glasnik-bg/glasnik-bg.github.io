What we build

Glasnik is a professional community platform. Members join through their employer and take part under a professional profile, so discussion about industries, roles and workplaces happens between people who do the work.

The platform is offered as a mobile application for iOS and Android alongside a web platform at glasnik.bg. Our first market is Bulgaria, followed by the wider region.

How we operate

European data residency

All personal data is processed and stored on infrastructure located in the European Union. ГЛАСНИК ООД (GLASNIK LTD OOD) is the data controller for that processing under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the GDPR) and Bulgarian data protection law, supervised by the Commission for Personal Data Protection (KZLD).
