Child Safety Standards

Effective 30 October 2026

These are the standards of Glasnik, the app and service operated by ГЛАСНИК ООД (GLASNIK LTD OOD), against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). They apply to everything on Glasnik: posts, comments, polls, reviews, guilds, usernames and profiles. They are part of our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Zero tolerance

Glasnik has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. It is prohibited to post, share, request, link to or promote, in any form:

  • child sexual abuse material, whether real, drawn, digitally altered or AI-generated;
  • content that sexualises minors, including sexualised comments about a child;
  • grooming: contacting or trying to build trust with a minor for sexual purposes;
  • sextortion, trafficking, or any solicitation of a minor;
  • anything that facilitates, normalises or advertises the abuse or exploitation of children.

There are no exceptions, including for "fiction", humour or claimed research.

Glasnik is for adults

Glasnik is a community for working professionals. You must be at least 18 years old to use it (Terms of Service), and members confirm a work email before they can post. The app is listed for adults only in the app stores. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it.

How to report

  • In the app: every post, comment and review has a Report option (the "⋯" menu on a post, or long-press a comment). Choose the reason that fits; reports about child safety are handled first.
  • By email: contact@glasnik.bg with the subject "Child safety". You don't need an account to report.

Please do not download, copy or forward suspected abuse material, even to show it to us. Tell us where it is; we will find it.

If a child is in immediate danger, call 112 (emergency number across the EU) first.

What we do

  • Remove the content as soon as we are aware of it. Child safety reports are reviewed as a priority, and in any case within 24 hours.
  • Ban the account permanently. Accounts that share or seek CSAM are not reinstated.
  • Preserve the relevant information securely for law enforcement, as the law allows.
  • Report to the competent authorities: the Bulgarian police (the cybercrime unit of the Chief Directorate for Combating Organised Crime, ГДБОП), and other national authorities or the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable.
  • Cooperate with lawful requests from law enforcement.

Before content is published, our system checks it for personal details and abusive language, and our moderators review every report. Members can also block anyone, which hides that member's content immediately.

Compliance with the law

Glasnik complies with the child-protection laws that apply to it, including Bulgarian law, the EU Digital Services Act (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065) and EU rules on combating the sexual abuse and sexual exploitation of children, and with the Google Play and Apple App Store policies on child safety.
