Deleting your Glasnik account

Effective 10 October 2026

This page is for the Glasnik app on the App Store and Google Play, made by ГЛАСНИК ООД (GLASNIK LTD OOD). You can delete your account and your data at any time, with or without the app. Below is how, and exactly what happens, including what we keep and why.

In the app

  1. Open Settings: on your Profile tab, tap the settings icon at the top right, or on Home tap ⋮ → Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  3. Read what will be removed, tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Deletion happens straight away, and you are signed out.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, or can't sign in, email us and we will delete the account for you.

Email a deletion request

  1. Write to privacy@glasnik.bg, ideally from the email address you sign in with. Tell us whether you signed in with email and password, Google or Apple.
  2. We confirm it's you. We send one email to the address on the account and ask you to reply to it. If you used Apple's Hide My Email, Apple forwards that email to you, and your reply goes back through Apple. You never need to tell us your real address.
  3. We delete the account within 30 days of your confirmation, usually within a few working days, and email you when it is done.

We never ask for your password, and we don't need your real name or a copy of any document.

What is deleted

  • Your login. Your sign-in account (email and password, or the link to your Google or Apple sign-in) is deleted. You will not be able to sign in again.
  • Your profile. Username, job role, employer, seniority, experience, avatar, bio and interests are erased.
  • Your work email verification. The stored hash and the employer domain are removed, together with your employment history, and your contribution to that organisation's verified-member count is reversed.
  • Your posts and comments. They are removed from the service and no longer visible to anyone, in any feed, guild or search.
  • Your reviews, and your agree/disagree votes on other members' reviews.
  • Your likes, poll votes and saved posts, and the counts they added to are corrected.
  • Your guild memberships. You leave every guild, and each guild's member count is corrected.
  • Your notifications and push tokens. Your notification list is deleted, and no phone receives Glasnik notifications for you again.
  • Your preferences and settings.

What we keep, and why

  • A residual account record with no personal data in it. Because content rows reference an internal identifier, we retain an emptied record so the database stays consistent. It contains no name, no email, no employer, and nothing that identifies you.
  • Backups. Deletion propagates to backups on our normal backup cycle, up to 30 days, after which no copy remains.
  • Records we are legally required to keep, such as those relating to a legal claim or a regulatory obligation, for as long as the law requires.
  • Aggregate statistics that cannot be linked to you — for example an organisation's overall rating, which is a number derived from many members.

Things worth knowing before you delete

  • It cannot be undone. There is no recovery period and no way to restore the account.
  • Your username is released and may become available to someone else.
  • You can register again later, but it will be a new account with no history.
  • Your work email can be verified again on a future account — verification follows whoever controls the mailbox.

If you only want to change something

You do not have to delete everything to fix one thing:

  • Change employer? Verify a new work email in Settings — no need to start over.
  • Want a post gone? Delete individual posts, comments or reviews and keep your account.
  • Want your data? Request a copy at privacy@glasnik.bg under your right of access.
  • Want fewer notifications? Turn push off, or choose which kinds you get, in Settings → Notifications.
  • Just need a break? Signing out deletes nothing, and turns off push on that phone.

Questions

privacy@glasnik.bg — we answer data requests within one month, as required by the GDPR.

You also have the right to complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (Комисия за защита на личните данни) or the data protection authority where you live.
