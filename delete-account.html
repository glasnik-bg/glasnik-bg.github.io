Deleting your Glasnik account

Effective 23 September 2026

You can delete your account and your data at any time. This page explains how, and exactly what happens — including what we keep and why.

In the app

Settings → Account → Delete account

You will be asked to confirm. Deletion begins immediately.

Without the app

If you cannot access the app, or have already removed it, email privacy@glasnik.bg from the address you registered with, asking us to delete your account.

We will verify that the request comes from the account holder and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner. We will confirm by email when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your login. Your authentication account, including your email address and password, is deleted. You will not be able to sign in again.
  • Your profile. Username, job role, employer, seniority, experience, avatar, bio and interests are erased.
  • Your work email verification. The stored hash and the employer domain are removed, and your contribution to that organisation's verified-member count is reversed.
  • Your preferences and settings.
  • Your posts and comments. They are removed from the service and no longer visible to anyone.
  • Your reviews, and your votes on other members' reviews.

What we keep, and why

  • A residual account record with no personal data in it. Because content rows reference an internal identifier, we retain an emptied record so the database stays consistent. It contains no name, no email, no employer, and nothing that identifies you.
  • Backups. Deletion propagates to backups on our normal backup cycle, up to 30 days, after which no copy remains.
  • Records we are legally required to keep, such as those relating to a legal claim or a regulatory obligation, for as long as the law requires.
  • Aggregate statistics that cannot be linked to you — for example an organisation's overall rating, which is a number derived from many members.

Things worth knowing before you delete

  • It cannot be undone. There is no recovery period and no way to restore the account.
  • Your username is released and may become available to someone else.
  • You can register again later, but it will be a new account with no history.
  • Your work email can be verified again on a future account — verification follows whoever controls the mailbox.

If you only want to change something

You do not have to delete everything to fix one thing:

  • Change employer? Verify a new work email in Settings — no need to start over.
  • Want a post gone? Delete individual posts, comments or reviews and keep your account.
  • Want your data? Request a copy at privacy@glasnik.bg under your right of access.
  • Want to stop emails? Turn off notifications in Settings.

Questions

privacy@glasnik.bg — we answer data requests within one month, as required by the GDPR.

You also have the right to complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (Комисия за защита на личните данни) or the data protection authority where you live.
