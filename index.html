What we do

The conversations you can't have in the office chat

Glasnik brings professionals together around the things they actually want to talk about: the state of their industry, how companies are to work for, and where careers go next. Every member has verified their employer, so the conversation is between people who genuinely do the work.

Communities

Guilds

Communities by profession, industry or city, so discussion stays useful to the people taking part. Ask the questions you would not put in a group chat your manager can read.

  • Join as many guilds as you like, or start your own.
  • Post to a guild, or to everyone on Glasnik.
  • Fellow members see your pseudonym, never your name.

How verification works

We prove where you work, not who you are

The hard part of an anonymous network is proving something true about someone while knowing as little about them as possible. This is how we do it.

1

You enter a work email

We send a one-time code to confirm you can receive mail at that address.

2

The address is set aside

We keep it only in protected form, with its domain, never alongside anything you post. Nobody else ever sees it.

3

Your employer is established, you are not

Other members see that a verified professional is speaking. They never see your name, your email or your account.

Our approach

A European product, built to keep you unidentifiable

Glasnik is built and operated in the European Union under the GDPR. We do not sell personal data or build an advertising business on it. Our Community Guidelines set out what is welcome, and organisations can raise concerns through our Notice and Action process. The full detail is in our Privacy Policy.
