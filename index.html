Glasnik is a professional community platform where people can discuss their work, their industry and their careers with colleagues across the country. We are building it in Sofia for the Bulgarian market, and then for the wider region.

What we do

Glasnik brings professionals together around the things they actually want to talk about — the state of their industry, what roles are worth, how companies are to work for, and where careers go next. Members join through their employer, so conversation happens between people who genuinely do the work.

Professional communities

Groups organised by industry, profession and city, so discussion stays useful to the people taking part.

Career and pay insight

Aggregated information on what roles pay across Bulgarian and regional markets, contributed by members and reported as ranges.

Workplace insight

Company information and reviews contributed by people who work in the industry, to help others make better career decisions.

Built for the region

Bulgarian language support and regional labour market data from the start, rather than a translation of something built elsewhere.

Our approach

Glasnik is a European product, built and operated in the European Union under the GDPR. Personal data stays within the EU, and we do not sell it or build an advertising business on it.

Members take part under a professional profile rather than a personal one, so people can speak openly about their industry. We set clear community standards and apply them — our Community Guidelines set out what is welcome and what is not, and organisations can raise concerns with us through the process described on our Notice and Action page.

How we handle personal data is set out in full in our Privacy Policy.
