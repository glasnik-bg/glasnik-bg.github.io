Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

Glasnik is a professional network where you can speak about your working life under a pseudonym, while other members can see that your employer has been verified. That design creates an unusual privacy obligation: we have to prove something about you without being able to reveal who you are. This policy explains exactly how we do that, and what we do and do not keep.

Controller. ГЛАСНИК ООД (GLASNIK LTD OOD), company number 208861127, registered at 18A Neofit Hilendarski str., Vitosha District, Sofia 1434, Bulgaria, is the controller of your personal data. Privacy contact: privacy@glasnik.bg.

1. The short version

  • We do not keep your work email address alongside anything you post. It is held separately and in protected form, together with its domain.
  • Your posts, comments and reviews are published under a pseudonym, never your real name.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app, and that deletion is real.

2. What we collect

2.1 Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address and a password, both handled by our authentication provider. We never see or store your password. If you sign in with Google or Apple we receive your email address and, where you allow it, your name.

2.2 Your profile

You may provide a username (a pseudonym you choose or we suggest), job role, employer name, seniority level, an experience band, an avatar selection, a short bio, and up to three interests. All of this is optional except the username, and all of it is chosen by you.

2.3 Work email verification — the important part

To post or comment, you verify that you control a work email address. When you do:

  • We send a one-time code to that address.
  • The address is held temporarily in memory (in our cache, for fifteen minutes) only so that the code can be matched to it.
  • On success we store the address in protected form and the domain part (for example example.com). The address itself is then discarded and never written to permanent storage.

This lets us confirm that a given address has already been verified. The domain lets us link you to an employer so that reviews come from people who actually work there. Neither can be reversed into your email address.

2.4 Content you create

Posts, comments, reviews, poll votes, likes, saved items and guild memberships. Reviews include the ratings, and the pros and cons text you write.

2.5 Technical data

Standard server logs including IP address and request metadata, retained for security and abuse prevention. We do not currently use analytics or advertising SDKs. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect, and where consent is required we will ask for it first.

2.6 Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to our own servers so we can fix it. No outside crash-reporting company is involved. A report contains the error and where in our code it happened, which type of screen you were on (for example "a guild page", never which one), your operating system version, on Android your phone model, and the app version. It is sent without your login, so it is not linked to your account, and it does not contain your email, username, the text of anything you are writing, or screenshots. Before storing a report we remove anything that looks like an email address, a code or a link parameter. Crash reports are kept with our server logs and used only to find and fix faults (our legitimate interest in a working, secure app).

2.7 What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health data, financial data, or any special category data under Article 9 of the GDPR. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

5. What other people can see

  • Your username, job role, employer, seniority and avatar appear on content you post, unless you post anonymously.
  • When you post anonymously, your username is hidden and your avatar is randomised. Your role and employer may still be shown, because that context is the point of the post. Please bear this in mind if you work somewhere small — a specific role at a small employer can identify a person.
  • Your email address, work email address, and internal identifiers are never shown to anyone.
  • Saved items are private and are never visible to other members.
  • Reviews show that the author is a verified employee of that organisation. Your role is shown only if you choose to reveal it.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on it.

  • Erasure is available directly in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. It removes your personal data, removes your content from the service, releases your work-email verification, and deletes your login. You can also request it at privacy@glasnik.bg — see our deletion page.
  • For any other request, contact privacy@glasnik.bg. We will respond within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the Bulgarian supervisory authority, the Commission for Personal Data Protection (Комисия за защита на личните данни), or to the authority in your country of residence.

8. Security

Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and never reach our servers. Work email addresses are held in protected form, separately from platform content. All traffic is encrypted in transit. Our database and cache sit in the European Union with access restricted to the application. Verification codes are single-use, expire quickly, are rate limited, and are never written to our logs.

9. Children

Glasnik is for working professionals and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact privacy@glasnik.bg and we will remove it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date and, for any change that materially affects your rights or introduces a new purpose for your data, tell you in the app before it takes effect.
